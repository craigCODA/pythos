## Scope Boundary

The first milestone may contain only:

```text
boot/
core/
shared/
scripts/
tests/
docs/
```

Do not add embedded Python, MicroPython, services, semantic storage, networking, audio beyond the Phase 6 QEMU AC97 target, widgets beyond the fixed Phase 5 proof, package management, AI, SMP, ring-3 applications, or broad hardware support.

## Unsafe Rust Policy

Every `unsafe` block must document:

1. the invariant being relied upon
2. who established that invariant
3. the permitted lifetime of the invariant
4. pointer ownership
5. expected alignment
6. expected mapped length
7. concurrency assumptions
8. consequences of violation

Keep unsafe regions small. Do not wrap large functions in `unsafe`.
